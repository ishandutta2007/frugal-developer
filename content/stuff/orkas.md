Orkas is an open-source, local-first desktop AI workforce. A Commander turns goals into executable plans and coordinates specialist agents in parallel or sequence through one chat.

Free core desktop application for research, coding, data analysis and documents.
MIT-licensed public source: https://github.com/Orkas-AI/Orkas
Packaged installers for macOS and Windows; Linux runs from source.
Model usage and optional services may cost extra.
